# Projects & Publications

## Open-Source Projects

### [Bonmin](https://coin-or.github.io/Bonmin/)
**Basic Open-source Nonlinear Mixed INteger programming** — COIN-OR

An open-source C++ solver for convex Mixed-Integer Nonlinear Programs (MINLP). Implements branch-and-bound (B-BB), outer approximation (B-OA), Quesada–Grossmann (B-QG), and a hybrid algorithm (B-Hyb).

- [Source on GitHub](https://github.com/pobonomo/Bonmin)
- License: EPL-1.0

### [gurobi-machinelearning](https://github.com/pobonomo/gurobi-machinelearning)
Insert trained machine-learning predictors directly into Gurobi optimization models (fork/contributor).
